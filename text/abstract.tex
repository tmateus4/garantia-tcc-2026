Product warranty tracking is a recurring demand in consumer relations, already widely addressed by applications aimed at the final consumer. However, there is a lack of dedicated and low-complexity tools aimed at small retailers and technical assistance providers, who frequently resort to inaccurate manual controls or oversized enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems. Given this scenario, this work proposes the development of a web system using a Micro-SaaS (Software as a Service) model focused exclusively on warranty control. The platform adopts a multi-tenant architecture, allowing different businesses to act independently in registering clients, products, and sales. The system enables the automated tracking of legal, contractual, and extended warranty periods, besides structuring the management of warranty claim requests, providing greater organization, data centralization, and operational efficiency for the micro-entrepreneur.
